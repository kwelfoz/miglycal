# Miglycal

**Your local music player**

Miglycal is an Android music player focused on your local music library. Keep your collection organised, explore your albums and artists, and enjoy your music without depending on a streaming service.

## 🌐 Features

- **Local music library** — Browse and play music stored on your device.
- **Music organisation** — Explore your collection through folders, albums, artists and playlists.
- **Metadata lookup** — Find additional information and artwork for your music online.
- **Lyrics** — View available song lyrics.
- **Search** — Find tracks, albums and artists across your library.

*Some features may depend on device compatibility or online metadata availability.*

## ⚙️ About

Miglycal is made around a simple idea: your music should remain yours.

No streaming subscription required. No need to upload your collection. Just your music on your device.

## 🛠️ Built with

- Android
- Kotlin
- Android Studio

## 📦 Installation

Download the latest available APK from the [Releases](https://github.com/kwelfoz/miglycal/releases) page.

Install it on your Android device and grant the necessary permissions to access your music files.
